PocketChords

A free app for playing and singing along with chord charts on piano or guitar. Available in English, Chinese and Japanese.

Get help

Email shawn.xiang2@gmail.com with your device model and what happened. Screenshots help.

Report abuse

To report a shared song, open its link in the app and choose Report. You can also block the person who shared it there. If you do not have the app, use the email address above and include the link.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Without an account, your songs stay on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google to keep them across devices.

How do I change the language? Tap the flag menu in the app and pick a language.

How do I delete my account? Open the account menu in the app and choose Delete Account. Your account and songs are removed.
