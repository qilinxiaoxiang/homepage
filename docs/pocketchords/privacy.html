PocketChords Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

PocketChords is a free app for playing and singing along with chord charts. It is run by Shawn Xiang, an individual developer. This page explains what the app stores and what you can do about it.

Using the app without an account

You can use PocketChords without signing in. Your songs and settings then stay on your device and are not sent to us.

What we store if you sign in

  • Your email address and the account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive only the relay address.
  • The display name your sign-in provider shares, if any.
  • The songs you save: titles, lyrics, chords and arrangement settings.

We use this information only to sign you in and to keep your songs in sync across your devices.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts accounts and songs on servers in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to that step.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and songs until you delete them. To delete your account, open the account menu in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and your songs from our live database right away. Copies in our daily backups are overwritten within 30 days.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

Children

PocketChords is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
