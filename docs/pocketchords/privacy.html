PocketChords Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

PocketChords is a free app for playing and singing along with chord charts. It is run by Shawn Xiang (legal name Zheng Xiang, as shown on the App Store), an individual developer. This page explains what the app stores and what you can do about it.

Using the app without an account

You can use PocketChords without signing in. Your songs and settings then stay on your device and are not sent to us.

What we store if you sign in

  • Your email address and the account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive only the relay address.
  • The display name your sign-in provider shares, if any.
  • The songs you save: titles, lyrics, chords and arrangement settings.

We use this information to sign you in, to keep your songs in sync across your devices, to deliver the songs you choose to share, and to review reports and enforce our Terms of Use. We do not use it for anything else.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts accounts and songs on servers in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to that step.

Supabase, Apple and Google each process this data only to provide their service to us, and each provides protection of your data equal to or stronger than what this policy describes.

Sharing songs by link

When you share a song by link, we store a copy of that song. Anyone who has the link can see the song's name on a web page, without an account. Your name and email are not shown. People who open it in the app get their own copy, and they can share that copy on. A copy someone has already taken stays with them, unless we remove it after upholding a report. Text is checked automatically when you share it, and text that breaks our Terms of Use is refused.

In the app you can report a shared song or block the person who shared it. We store your report, with its reason and your account, so that we can review it, and a link reported by three different people stops working automatically. We store your blocks so that the person's links stop opening for you, and the person you block is not told. You can clear your blocks in the app. Your reports and blocks are removed when you delete your account.

Connecting an AI tool

You can choose to connect an AI tool, such as Claude, to your PocketChords account. Nothing is connected unless you approve it on the permission screen.

  • Once you allow it, that tool can read and edit your songs under the permission you granted. It cannot delete your songs or your account.
  • Your songs pass to that tool only because you asked for it. The tool is a separate service that you choose, not one of our processors, and its own privacy policy applies to what it does with them.
  • You can remove a tool's access at any time from Connected Apps in the app's account menu, or on the connected apps page. It can no longer connect, and any access it still holds ends within an hour.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and songs until you delete them. When you delete a song, its content is removed from our live database within 30 days. When a shared link stops, the copy it held is removed within 90 days. Reports stay for as long as the reporter's account exists. To delete your account, open the account menu in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and your songs from our live database right away. Copies in our daily backups are overwritten within 30 days.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

Children

PocketChords is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
