PocketChords Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

PocketChords is a free app for playing and singing along with chord charts. It is run by Shawn Xiang (legal name Zheng Xiang, as shown on the App Store), an individual developer. This page explains what the app stores and what you can do about it.

Using the app without an account

You can use PocketChords without signing in. Your songs and settings then stay on your device and are not sent to us.

What we store if you sign in

  • Your email address and the account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive only the relay address.
  • The display name your sign-in provider shares, if any.
  • The songs you save: titles, lyrics, chords and arrangement settings.
  • If you use the assistant: how many requests your account has made, and for up to 7 days each request and its result.
  • If you tap I want more to ask for more assistant requests: your account identifier, email address and when you tapped.
  • If you report an assistant answer: the answer, your request, your reason and your account.

We use this information to sign you in, to keep your songs in sync across your devices, to deliver the songs you choose to share, to answer your assistant requests and count them against your free allowance, to learn whether people want more requests, and to review reports and enforce our Terms of Use. We do not use it for anything else.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts accounts and songs on servers in the United States.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts our assistant server in the United States.
  • Anthropic's Claude writes the assistant's answers. See "The assistant" below for what it receives.
  • When you send a song to a WeChat friend, the app hands the card to the WeChat app through Tencent's WeChat OpenSDK, and WeChat delivers it. The SDK may contact Tencent's servers to check the share settings. Tencent's own privacy policy applies to WeChat.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to that step.
  • When someone reports a shared song, Google's Gmail delivers the report to us. The email contains the song's name, an excerpt of its lyrics, the reason given and the sender's account identifier. A report of an assistant answer reaches us the same way, with the answer, the request, the reason and the reporter's account identifier.

Supabase, AWS, Anthropic, Tencent (for the WeChat OpenSDK), Apple and Google each process this data only to provide their service to us, and each provides protection of your data equal to or stronger than what this policy describes.

Sharing songs by link

When you share a song by link, we store a copy of that song. Anyone who has the link can see the song's name on a web page, without an account. Your name and email are not shown. People who open it in the app get their own copy, and they can share that copy on. A copy someone has already taken stays with them, unless we remove it after upholding a report. Text is checked automatically when you share it, and text that breaks our Terms of Use is refused.

In the app you can report a shared song or block the person who shared it. We store your report, with its reason and your account, so that we can review it, and a link reported by three different people stops working automatically. We store your blocks so that the person's links stop opening for you, and the person you block is not told. You can clear your blocks in the app. Your blocks are removed when you delete your account.

The assistant

The app has an assistant that can find chords for a song, change its key or rearrange it. It is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The first time you open it, the app asks for your permission, and nothing is sent until you agree.

  • When you send a request, our server passes your request text, the open song (title, key, lyrics and chords) and your earlier conversation with the assistant about that song to Anthropic. To find chords, Claude may search the web and open chord chart pages.
  • Your email address, name and account identifier are not sent to Anthropic.
  • To check chords against the original recording, the assistant may send the song's title, artist and lyric lines to our own computer, which finds the song's public recording, analyses it and returns only the chords and their timing. That computer keeps the recording and its analysis for up to 7 days, deletes your lyric lines as soon as they have been lined up with the recording, and keeps that line-up for up to 7 days.
  • Anthropic processes this data only to give us the answer and does not use it to train its models. Under its commercial terms it keeps this data only for a limited time.
  • Our server keeps each request and its result for up to 7 days so that a result can be picked up again, then deletes it. It keeps a count of requests per account so that the free allowance works, and the I want more taps and answer reports described above. These stay until you delete your account, and deleting your account in the app erases them first. A report email already delivered to us stays in our mailbox so that we can handle that report; deleting your account does not delete it.
  • Every answer passes the same automatic text filter as shared songs before you see it, and an answer the filter refuses changes nothing. You can report any answer with Report this answer; we read every report.
  • If you do not want this, do not use the assistant. Everything else in the app works without it.

Connecting an AI tool

You can choose to connect an AI tool, such as Claude, to your PocketChords account. Nothing is connected unless you approve it on the permission screen.

  • Once you allow it, that tool can read and edit your songs under the permission you granted. It cannot delete your songs or your account.
  • Your songs pass to that tool only because you asked for it. The tool is a separate service that you choose, not one of our processors, and its own privacy policy applies to what it does with them.
  • You can remove a tool's access at any time from Connected Apps in the app's account menu, or on the connected apps page. It can no longer connect, and any access it still holds ends within an hour.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and songs until you delete them. When you delete a song, its content is removed from our live database within 30 days. A link you already made keeps its own copy until you stop it, which you can do at any time under My Shared Links in the account menu. When a shared link stops, the copy it held and its title are removed within 90 days. A report is removed when the account of the person who made it, or of the person who shared the song, is deleted. If we close an account for abuse, we keep its record and songs so that it stays closed, until the person asks us by email to delete them. To delete your account, open the account menu in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and your songs from our live database right away. Copies in our daily backups are overwritten within 30 days.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

Children

PocketChords is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.
